Cricket Run-Out Game

You're fielding, ball in hand, and the batter is scrambling for a run. Pick a throw speed and release angle to fire the ball at the stumps. Your arm can only generate so much speed — go too hard at the wrong angle and the ball sails over; too soft and it bounces short. The trajectory stays hidden until you commit, so you'll need to work out the physics rather than just eyeball it — and even a clean hit doesn't count if the ball arrives after the batter's already made their ground, so speed matters as much as accuracy.
