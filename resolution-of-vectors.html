Resolution of a Vector Into Components

Any vector can be split into perpendicular parts along the x and y axes. Set R's magnitude and angle, then step through dropping perpendiculars to read off Rx and Ry.

Vector R mag 0.00 · θ 0°
Rx = R cos θ 0.00
Ry = R sin θ 0.00

Vector R

  1. Place vector R at angle θ, tail at the origin.
  2. Drop perpendiculars from R's tip onto the x-axis and y-axis.
  3. Read off Rx and Ry — the components with R = Rx + Ry.
Rx = R cos θ, Ry = R sin θ
