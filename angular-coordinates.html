circular motion — angular coordinate system

A second way to say where something is

So far, position has meant an (x, y) pair. For anything moving on a circle, there's a far more natural description: how far around it has gone, measured as a single angle. Pick a reference direction, agree on a positive sense of rotation, and suddenly a whole circle's worth of positions collapses into one number, θ.

Angular position, θ

The angle between a reference direction (almost always the positive x-axis) and the line from the center to the object. By convention, counterclockwise is positive.

Radians

The natural unit for angles in physics — defined so that an angle of 1 rad subtends an arc exactly as long as the radius. A full circle is 2π rad.

θ(rad) = θ(°) × π/180

Arc length

The actual distance travelled along the circle connects directly to θ, as long as θ is measured in radians.

s = rθ
