Dot Product — Projection Intuition

A·B is |A| times the "shadow" B casts on A. Set A and B, then step through the projection and the rectangle whose area equals the dot product — and watch it trace a cosine curve as B swings around.

  1. Place A along the x-axis, and B at angle θ from A.
  2. Drop a perpendicular from B's tip onto A — that shadow is |B| cos θ.
  3. Shade the rectangle: sides |A| and the shadow. Its signed area is A·B.
