Triangle Law of Vector Subtraction

A − B is really A + (−B). Set the two vectors, then step through reversing B and placing it tip-to-tail with A to build the resultant.

  1. Place vector A in standard position, tail at the origin.
  2. Reverse B to get −B — same length, opposite direction.
  3. Slide −B so its tail sits at the tip of A.
  4. Draw R from A's tail to −B's tip. That's A − B.
R = A − B = A + (−B)
