kinematics — acceleration

A push doesn't always mean speeding up

Acceleration is the rate at which velocity itself changes. A positive number doesn't automatically mean "faster" — it depends entirely on which way the object is already moving. Build a motion below and watch the same acceleration speed the car up in one moment and slow it down in the next.

Acceleration

The rate of change of velocity — how quickly the velocity arrow grows, shrinks, or turns. Like velocity, it's a vector: it has a size and a direction.

acceleration = Δvelocity ⁄ Δtime

Speeding up, or slowing down?

Compare the direction of acceleration to the direction of velocity — not just the sign of the number.

speeding up velocity and acceleration point the same way
slowing down velocity and acceleration point opposite ways

Practice

Work these by hand, then check yourself. Take "up" or "forward" as positive unless told otherwise.

Problem 1 — concept check

A ball is thrown straight up and is still rising, with upward taken as positive. What are the signs of its velocity and acceleration, and is it speeding up or slowing down?

Problem 2

A car speeds up from 12 m/s to 28 m/s in 4.0 s, moving in a straight line the whole time. Find its average acceleration.

m/s²
Show solution
Average acceleration is the change in velocity over the time it took: (28 − 12) ⁄ 4.0 = 4.0 m/s².

Problem 3

A car moving at +20 m/s brakes with a constant acceleration of −4 m/s². Find how long it takes to come to rest, and its velocity 6.0 s after braking begins.

s
m/s
Show solution
It reaches rest when v = 20 + (−4)t = 0, so t = 5.0 s. The braking acceleration doesn't stop there — at t = 6.0 s, v = 20 + (−4)(6) = −4 m/s. The car has reversed direction: the same constant acceleration first slowed it down, then sped it up again backward.

Problem 4

An object moving at −8 m/s undergoes a constant acceleration of +2 m/s² for 3.0 s. Find its final velocity, and state whether it was speeding up or slowing down over that interval.

m/s
Show solution
Final velocity: −8 + (2)(3.0) = −2 m/s. Velocity stayed negative the entire 3 seconds while acceleration was positive — opposite signs the whole way — so the object was slowing down throughout, from 8 m/s down to 2 m/s (it never reached zero, so it never started speeding up again).
